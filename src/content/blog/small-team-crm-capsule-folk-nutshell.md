## Start with how you sell

A small services team usually picks a CRM by feature list. We start with a different question: does your pipeline come from relationships you already have, or from leads you have to work?

The answer points to a different tool.

## Capsule: the simple first CRM

Capsule keeps contacts, sales pipelines and tasks in a clean interface, and it has a free plan for very small teams. It is the one we suggest when the goal is simply to stop losing track of deals.

## folk: for relationship-driven sales

folk is built for founders and agencies whose work comes from their network. It collects contacts from LinkedIn and email and reminds you to follow up, which suits a pipeline built on introductions and repeat clients.

## Nutshell: when you need automation

Nutshell adds automated follow-ups and email marketing to the pipeline. It fits a team that already has a steady flow of inbound leads and wants the CRM to chase them.

## How we decide

1. **Fewer than ten open deals** Capsule. Keep it simple until the process is stable.
2. **Work comes from your network** folk. Its contact capture and reminders match how you sell.
3. **Steady inbound leads** Nutshell. The follow-up automation pays for itself.

Whichever you pick, set up the pipeline stages and the required fields before you import contacts. Prices for all three are in the side panel and come from our Market catalogue.
